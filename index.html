Core Focus

Leverage Score Sampling for Accelerated Diffusion Inference

Developing importance sampling strategies based on statistical leverage scores to reduce the number of discretization steps required during reverse diffusion. My work formalizes the connection between leverage scores of the score network Jacobian and per-timestep contribution to the evidence lower bound, enabling adaptive step selection that preserves sample fidelity under fixed compute budgets.

#LeverageScores #ImportanceSampling #DiffusionModels #ScoreMatching #ELBO
New Direction

Flow Matching & Continuous Normalizing Flows

Investigating conditional flow matching and rectified flow as an alternative to stochastic diffusion, leveraging the deterministic ODE formulation for cheaper likelihood evaluation. I am particularly interested in how leverage-score-guided sampling extends from SDEs to the flow matching objective, and how optimal transport couplings influence sample complexity in the low-NFE regime.

Theory

Sampling Theory for Score-Based Generative Models

Analyzing convergence rates and sample complexity of discretized reverse-time SDEs and probability-flow ODEs. Current directions include variance-reduced estimators for the score function, and the role of leverage scores in bounding discretization error under non-uniform step schedules.

#SDE #PF-ODE #ConvergenceRates #SampleComplexity
UQ

Calibrated Uncertainty Quantification for Generative Models

Building distribution-free, calibrated uncertainty estimates for samples produced by diffusion and flow matching models. Combining conformal prediction with Bayesian treatments of the score network to yield valid prediction sets and confidence intervals over generated outputs — a prerequisite for deployment in scientific and clinical pipelines.

#ConformalPrediction #BayesianDL #Calibration #ReliableAI
Applications

Scientific Applications of Efficient Generative Models

Applying efficient diffusion and flow matching samplers to molecular generation, protein backbone design, and climate downscaling. Leverage-score-guided inference reduces wall-clock cost for high-dimensional scientific sampling without sacrificing physical validity.
